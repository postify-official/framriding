Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Framride collects through its passenger and driver apps and this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using Framride you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Account details. Name, email address, phone number and password (stored in protected form). Passengers and drivers can verify their account with a one-time code.

Location. Passengers share their location to set a pickup point and follow a trip. Drivers share their location while online so nearby passengers can be matched and trips can be tracked.

Driver and fleet information. Drivers may provide identity details, driving licence information and vehicle details. Fleet owners may add drivers and vehicles to their fleet account.

Trip and messages. Pickup and drop-off points, route, trip times, status, and in-app messages between passenger and driver.

Device and usage data. Device type, app version, crash reports and notification tokens needed to send ride alerts.

How we use information

We use your information to create and secure your account, match passengers with drivers, run and display trips, enable in-app chat, send ride and account notifications, support fleet management, prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

When you book a ride, your driver sees your name, pickup and destination, and the driver's name, vehicle and live location are shown to you. Fleet owners can see trip activity of drivers in their own fleet. We use trusted service providers for hosting, authentication, notifications and payment processing, and they handle data only to provide those services. We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the safety of users.

Location permissions

Location is used only to provide ride features. You can turn it off in your device settings, but booking or accepting rides will not work without it.

Retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements and resolve disputes. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to close your account, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local data protection law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Framride is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
