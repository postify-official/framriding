Three ways to drive with Framride

Pick the setup that fits how you work. Each one uses the same driver app, with tools built for that role.

Independent driver

Use your own vehicle and drive when you choose.

  • Go online and receive nearby ride requests
  • Accept or decline each trip
  • Track your trips and earnings

Fleet driver

Drive a vehicle that belongs to a fleet owner.

  • Join a fleet with an invitation
  • Receive ride requests like any driver
  • Trips are linked to your fleet

Fleet owner

Manage several vehicles and drivers from one account.

  • Add and manage your drivers and vehicles
  • See trips completed across your fleet
  • Keep your fleet separate from other owners

Riding with Framride

Everything you expect from a ride booking app, in a simple flow.

Book in a few taps

Set a pickup and destination, choose your location on the map or use your current one.

Nearby drivers

Your request goes out to available drivers close to you.

Live trip tracking

Watch your driver arrive and follow the trip on the map.

Chat with your driver

Message your driver in the app during a ride, without sharing your number.

Clear trip details

See your route and trip history after every ride.

Account security
